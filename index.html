How it works

Your camera counts.
Your city grows.

Stand your iPhone on the floor facing you and start a set. RepCity watches your form and only counts full reps: all the way down, all the way up. Every rep pays coins, and the coins are the only way to build.

Prop it up

No gear, no wearable.

Just your iPhone and a bit of floor. Push-ups, squats and plank, with the counter and a coin for every rep on screen.

Earn as you go

Coins for reps, gems for habits.

Reps pay coins. Guided workouts pay over double, and streaks and records pay gems for special decorations.

Stays on your phone

Nothing is filmed.

Reps are counted on your iPhone, frame by frame. Video is never saved and never uploaded.

02
The city

Day 1 is an empty island.

Place homes, shops and parks, lay roads, build bridges and buy new land. Homes on a road fill up with residents. Your best sets become monuments that turn bronze, silver and gold, and a time-lapse plays your whole city back, day by day.

Skip a week and nothing breaks. The city waits for your next rep.

03
Goals

From your first 20 to 100 a day.

100 a Day is a guided plan: a few sets with a minute's rest, five more push-ups every time you finish, until you do 100 in a day. Days in a row pay gems.

The 1-Minute Test shows how many push-ups you can do in 60 seconds, from Starter to Machine, and sets where your plan begins.

Guided workouts

14 quick timed workouts.

Race the clock, do reps every minute or climb a little higher each round. Hard mode when you're ready.

Ranks

Weekly leaderboards.

Sign in with Game Center to see where your reps rank this week, and visit other players' cities.

Apple Health

Every workout saved.

With your OK, workouts go to Apple Health with their reps, time and estimated calories.
