Privacy policy

How information is used when you play RepCity or contact us for help.

1. Who we are

RepCity is provided by TouchstoneTech ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy covers the RepCity app for iPhone and this website. For privacy questions, contact contacttouchstonetech@gmail.com.

You don't need to create a RepCity account to play. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number in the app. Some features use services from Apple and Google, described below.

2. In short

Your camera video never leaves your iPhone. Your workouts and city are saved in your own iCloud, which we can't see. If you use Game Center, your display name, city and scores are visible to other players. We collect usage statistics to improve the game (in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland only if you allow it) and crash reports to fix crashes. We don't sell your information, and the app shows no ads.

3. The camera

The front camera is used only while you work out, to recognise your body position and count your reps. Each video frame is analysed in your iPhone's memory and then discarded. Video and photos are never saved, never uploaded and never seen by us.

4. Your game data

Saved in your private iCloud

Your workouts, coins, gems and city are saved on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud storage, so they're available on your other devices and after reinstalling. This data is in your iCloud account and we can't access it.

Only on your iPhone

Your sound and reminder settings, the players you've blocked and the cities you've visited recently stay on your iPhone.

Height and weight (optional)

If you enter them, they're used only on your iPhone to estimate the calories your workouts burn, and saved with your game in your private iCloud. They're never sent to us and never shown to other players.

5. Public profile and leaderboards

Game Center is optional. If you sign in:

  • Apple gives the app your Game Center player ID and nickname.
  • Your display name (and a lower-case version of it used for search), number of residents, residents gained this week and the layout of your city are stored in the app's public iCloud database (run by Apple), so other players can see you on the leaderboards, find you by name and visit your city. Your profile is identified by a scrambled (hashed) version of your Game Center player ID. It's updated shortly after your city changes, so others can tell roughly when you last played.
  • Your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards.

If you report another player, the report (their profile, the reason and your scrambled player ID) is stored so we can review it. Blocking a player is saved only on your iPhone.

6. Usage statistics

To learn how RepCity is played and what to improve, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. When usage statistics are on, it receives:

  • which screens you open and how long you stay on them;
  • what you do in the game, for example workouts finished (exercise, reps and coins earned), guided workouts and goals finished, buildings built, moved or sold, coins and gems spent, and purchases started or completed;
  • a random ID created for the app on your iPhone. It isn't your name, Game Center ID or Apple ID, and it isn't the advertising identifier: RepCity doesn't use the advertising identifier at all;
  • your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, language and approximate region (worked out from your network connection, not from GPS).

Your choice: if your iPhone's region is set to a country in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, RepCity asks you first and collects no usage statistics unless you tap Allow. In other regions they're on by default. Wherever you are, you can turn them on or off at any time under Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics in the app.

7. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (also a Google service) sends a report with what the app was doing at the time (for example, which screen was open), your iPhone model, iOS version and a random installation ID. Crash reports are always on, because we need them to keep the app working.

Camera images, your height and weight, health data and payment details are never sent to Google or to us.

8. Apple Health

If you allow it, RepCity saves each workout (type, time, duration, reps and, if you entered your height and weight, the estimated active calories) to Apple Health on your iPhone. RepCity never reads your Health data, and nothing from Apple Health is sent to us or used for analytics or advertising.

9. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple which purchases you have, so it can unlock them.

11. Your choices

  • Game Center is optional. Without it, nothing about you is public.
  • Usage statistics: Settings → Privacy → Share usage statistics.
  • Apple Health, camera and notifications: change them any time in the iPhone's Settings app.

Legal bases (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland): running the game you asked for, including your saved game, Game Center and purchases (contract); usage statistics in these regions (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings); crash reports and reviewing reports about other players (our legitimate interest in a working, safe app).

12. Retention and deletion

  • Your game stays in your iCloud until you delete it. In the app, Settings → Delete account deletes your city, workouts, coins, gems and public profile from your iPhone and iCloud. Delete it while you're online and signed in to Game Center so the public profile goes too; if it doesn't, email us and we'll remove it. Scores already sent to Game Center and workouts saved in Apple Health are kept by Apple; you can remove them in the Game Center and Health settings.
  • Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Reports about players are kept as long as needed to review them.

Turning off usage statistics stops new ones. To have past statistics deleted, email us.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, get a copy of it, object to or restrict how it's used, and withdraw consent. Email contacttouchstonetech@gmail.com. Because usage statistics aren't linked to your name, it helps if you tell us roughly when you installed the app. If you're in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

14. Children

RepCity is intended for ages 13 and up and isn't directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app's online features, email us and we'll delete their public profile.

15. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, ads, cookies or forms. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. Fonts on the home page are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

16. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app.

17. Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy: contacttouchstonetech@gmail.com
